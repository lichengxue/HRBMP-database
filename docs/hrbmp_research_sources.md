# HRBMP research and publication sources

Reviewed November 23, 2026. The static Research page uses the official project
names from sponsor or institutional award records. Its titles identify the
research topics; concise objective bullets summarize the documented scope.
Funding agencies and confirmed PI/co-PI roles are displayed without award-partner
fields, routine budget details, or repeated overview paragraphs.

## Funding and dates

The Research menu opens separate Current Research and Previous Research tabs.
Within each, projects run newest to oldest by documented project start or award
announcement date. Year-only dates retain their precision and follow dated
awards from the same year. Undated foundational projects are placed last;
a publication date is not substituted for an award date.

The 2025 microplastics award is confirmed, but neither the public records nor
the user confirm whether it remains active. At the user’s request it remains in the Current Projects portfolio, labeled
only as a 2025 seed award. This editorial placement is not independent
confirmation of an active award period; no end date or extension is invented.

## FJS funding chronology

The survey's restart and the dates of an individual Stony Brook contract are
different facts. The public heading now says **Resumed June 2023**.

- [NYSDEC](https://dec.ny.gov/nature/animals-fish-plants/hudson-delaware-marine-fisheries/hudson-river-fish-community-and-ecosystem-monitoring) confirms that it restarted the FJS in 2023 through Normandeau and the Chen Lab.
- [Normandeau's account](https://www.normandeau.com/legacy-project-leads-to-continued-surveying-on-the-hudson-river/) explicitly attributes 2023 and 2024 survey funding to NYSDEC, and new 2025 funding to the Hudson River Foundation / HREMP.
- [Stony Brook's active contract record](https://researchconnect.stonybrook.edu/en/projects/hudson-river-fall-juvenile-survey-2/) identifies Yong Chen as PI and Normandeau as the direct sponsor, April 1, 2025–March 31, 2027. These are contract dates, not the date the survey resumed. The user requested omission of the award-partner field from the GUI.
- The [earlier SBU contract](https://you.stonybrook.edu/somas/2023/11/30/november-2023-news-and-press-from-somas/) covered April 15, 2023–January 31, 2025. Its budget is not assigned to the newer award.

The Foundation's general HREMP page also refers to DEC-funded fisheries surveys
in its 2024–2026 monitoring description. This is broader program context, not a
clear annual attribution for the current SBU contract. The GUI therefore follows
the explicit partner account for 2023–2025 and does not infer who exclusively
funds 2026 or 2027. Normandeau is not presented as the original funding agency.

## Roles, objectives, and scope

The user identified Katrina Rokosz as the FJS calibration research lead. This is
not evidence of a co-PI appointment. Following the user's later request to show
only funding agencies and PI/co-PI roles, no additional research-lead or partner
fields are displayed. Likewise, research collaborators are not promoted to
co-PIs without evidence. The heavy-metal project's Cancer Center collaboration
is user-confirmed but is not used to identify an additional funding agency.

Objective bullets are concise editorial summaries, not verbatim quotations or
claims that the proposed work has been completed. The digital archive is a pilot;
it is not represented as having digitized all 50 million specimens. The
heavy-metal objectives remain broad because the available confirmed project
information is its funded title and team confirmation.

Only approved project details are taken from the CV. The CV itself, private
budgets, and pending proposals are not copied into the repository. Additional
unnamed PFAS awards remain unverified and are not invented or duplicated.
Program-level HREMP totals and the Entergy collection gift are not presented as
individual project awards. The official FIF program graphic was found on the President's website. The
user subsequently requested removal of all funder graphics from the research
pages; they identify funding agencies in text only. A separate homepage
Funding and Support grid identifies the documented agencies and programs,
with Normandeau acknowledged under Collaborators. Entergy is labeled for its
collection gift and foundational support, not as a new project grant.

The bibliography uses year sections from newest to oldest, then descending
journal publication dates within each year. Month-only dates retain their
original precision. The related framework paper is labeled within its year
section and uses the publisher’s November 3, 2026 online-first date.
Citation years use the journal volume when assigned. Accepted/in-press and
online-first records are marked accordingly. DOI identifiers are unique;
a DOI's embedded year alone is not a publication date. Three manuscript citations
are included at the user's explicit request in a separate section before the
published year groups. Their titles and author lists come from
the latest user-provided CV. On November 23, 2026, the user confirmed that
the metal-contamination review is **under revision** and the end-to-end
ecosystem-model paper and the monitoring-closure manuscript are **under review**. No publication dates or DOIs are
assigned to these manuscripts; the private CV and manuscript files are not
published. An ACS 2026 PFAS presentation is a conference
contribution, not evidence of an additional peer-reviewed journal article.
Unrelated Gulf of Maine and Chinese-estuary studies were screened out.

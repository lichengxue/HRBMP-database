# Homepage acknowledgement graphics

Retrieved November 23, 2026. These original graphics are used only for homepage
acknowledgements; Current Research and Previous Research remain free of logos.
The files are stored unchanged, displayed without cropping or recoloring, and
white marks use dark backgrounds. The FIF image is the official program banner.
All marks remain the property of their respective organizations.

## Credit categories

- Institutional partners identify the university and its SoMAS school. The school
  caption uses its current name, announced by Stony Brook in November 2026.
- Funding and Support acknowledges monitoring, archival development, and related
  HRBMP research. Role captions distinguish the federal funder (NOAA), program
  administrator (New York Sea Grant), seed-award programs, and collection donor.
  These credits do not imply that every organization funded this website.
- Normandeau Associates is a survey collaborator. Its direct SBU contract is not
  presented as evidence that it is the original government or foundation funder.

The FJS funding attribution remains NYSDEC in 2023–2024 and HRF/HREMP in 2025,
as described by Normandeau. Research-award evidence is documented in
`docs/hrbmp_research_sources.md`. Entergy's collection gift and seed support are
confirmed in Stony Brook's 2019 collection-gift announcement and SoMAS strategic
plan. Medicine seed support is based on the user-approved funded-project CV.
